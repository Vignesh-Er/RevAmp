## 3. Partner and Employer Credibility Architecture
When a formal partner network is under construction, early-stage platforms must leverage their ecosystem to build institutional trust. Digimation Flight will establish a **"Global Tech Ecosystem & Tool Partners"** grid using these 6 categorized partner types:

1.  **Cloud & Infrastructure Partners:** GitHub Education, MongoDB Atlas (Startup Program), and AWS Activate — showcasing modern stack alignment.
2.  **Marketing & Telemetry Integration:** HubSpot Academy (B2B educational partner) and Brevo (automated messaging tools).
3.  **Security & Infrastructure Frameworks:** OWASP Foundation (academic affiliate) for Cyber Security programs.
4.  **Academic Cohort Partners:** Private engineering colleges in Uttar Pradesh where Digimation Flight conducts official campus drives.
5.  **Industry Standards Alignment:** National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) alignment pathways (NEP 2020 compliance).
6.  **Enterprise Open Source Tools:** Docker, Kubernetes, and TensorFlow integration tracks as certified course curriculum frameworks.
