## 2. Production Audio Notes for Presenter
*   **Pacing:** Keep a steady, confident, and professional pace (around 130 to 140 words per minute). Avoid speaking too rapidly during code sections; focus instead on highlighting the business value.
*   **Tone:** Sound authoritative, technically competent, and mission-driven. When highlighting DPDP compliance and the child privacy gate, use a serious, deliberate tone to emphasize legal defensibility.
*   **Emphasis Words:** Place subtle vocal emphasis on metric terms: *1,250+ careers, 91.2% transition, 14 programmatic checks, and strict DPDP Act compliance.*
*   **Micro-interactions Showcase:** During visual transitions, ensure the screen captures subtle Framer Motion spring dampening transitions to illustrate the visual polish of the application.
